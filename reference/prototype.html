Fusion primer

Fusion combines light atoms such as deuterium and tritium at temperatures around 150 million °C, releasing energy. Fission splits heavy atoms instead.

Benefits: no carbon emissions in operation, much less long-lived radioactive waste than fission, but it is extremely hard to sustain.
