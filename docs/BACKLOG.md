# Backlog

## Epic: Overview

### Must

- Overview page exists with hero, key figures, brief cards and route-through guidance.
- The hero uses the approved wording and colour palette.
- The glossary contains the required terms and the fusion-specific additions.

### Acceptance criteria

- Sections appear in the approved order.
- The page links to the supply chain and why-now sections.
- Figures and labels follow the prototype wording.

## Epic: Fusion primer

### Must

- Primer page exists with fusion basics, cutaway reactor, project builders and the financing section.
- The cutaway has hover/focus states and a text fallback.
- Facts for companies and financing stay within the approved dataset.

### Acceptance criteria

- Each layer has a detail card with a material-specific link.
- The page remains readable on mobile and in print.

## Epic: Supply chain

### Must

- Material switcher and chain focus mode are implemented.
- Evidence tabs are present: Where, Who’s affected and Evidence.
- Audience lens chips work without hiding information.
- “Copy link to this stage” is present.

### Acceptance criteria

- Deep links work for material and stage anchors.
- The page remains accessible across screen sizes.

## Epic: Why now

### Must

- The page includes three reason columns, a rail timeline and the audience filter.
- The “Today” marker is computed from the current date.

### Acceptance criteria

- The timeline includes the fork at first-plant procurement.
- The page matches the wider site design language.

## Epic: Evidence and analysis

### Must

- Risk register page with sortable table and CSV export.
- Governance gaps page with a matrix and explanatory text.
- Demand page with honest data-gap cards and a lithium-6 visual.

### Acceptance criteria

- Rows show material, stage, confidence and source.
- The matrix clearly states Covered / Partly / Not covered with text labels.

## Epic: Remedy-first framework

### Must

- Ladder with five steps and guardrails.
- Scenario mode and ditch-and-run explanation.

### Acceptance criteria

- The page is linked from each brief.
- It avoids the unsupported claim that named fusion companies have used conflict-sourced material.

## Epic: Briefs

### Must

- Investor, EU and UN brief pages exist.
- Each follows the same template.
- Each contains print and copy-link actions.

### Acceptance criteria

- Each brief has an audience-specific ask and copyable actions.
- Content remains tied to the approved evidence.

## Epic: Methodology, limitations and sources

### Must

- Methodology, limitations and sources pages exist.
- About page contains the iteration log and AI-use disclosure.

### Acceptance criteria

- The site clearly differentiates between exposure and wrongdoing.
- Footer links work across the site.

## Epic: Research

### Must for Phase 2

- Replace news citations with primary UN and NGO material.
- Add missing country shares and traceability detail.
- Add smelter mapping and final demand estimates.

## Epic: Quality

### Must

- Accessibility and mobile checks.
- Print stylesheet validation.
- Keyboard and touch support for dropdowns and interactive controls.

### Acceptance criteria

- All interactive elements work by keyboard and touch.
- Headings and labels remain sentence case.

## Priority summary

- Must: overview, primer, supply chain, why now, evidence, remedy, briefs, methodology, limitations, sources, about and quality.
- Should: more detailed filters, stage explanation and deeper source mapping.
- Could: regional maps, demand sizing charts and brief translations.

## Summary

The backlog is phased. The first cycle delivers the full static site with honest gaps, the three briefs and evidence routines. The second cycle fills the missing data and improves the evidence behind the public-facing story.
